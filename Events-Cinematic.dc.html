Weddings & Private Events

Say it with the water behind you.

The renovated clubhouse and its waterfront rooms host weddings, rehearsal dinners, showers, corporate outings, and milestone celebrations — with our events team handling every detail.

Every gathering is built around what you want it to be — we will customize the menu and the experience so that it is extraordinary.

The Kitchen

A kitchen sized for the whole room.

Part of the renovation was a complete overhaul of the club's kitchen into a state-of-the-art facility, expanded by 1,500 square feet — built so a large-scale event and the evening's regular dinner service can run at the same time, with neither one compromised.

In practice that means a full-scale reception is held to the same standard as a Saturday night in the Formal Dining Room.

Member Traditions

Where memories are made, year after year.

Summer Celebrations

The Fourth of July BBQ, Memorial and Labor Day on the lawn, Dive-In Cinema at the pool — and a fireworks night all its own over the water.

Signature Tournaments

The Willie Park Jr Invitational, the Pine Member-Guest, the Bear Member-Member, the Willie Cup and the Club Championship.

Nights Out

The Kentucky Derby Party, Comedy Night, Claws & Cocktails, the Concert Series, and sunset cruises when the day winds down.

Holiday Traditions

Halloween Spooktakular, the Holiday Boutique, Gingerbread Brunch, Thanksgiving dinner, Santa Brunch and the Holiday Ball.
