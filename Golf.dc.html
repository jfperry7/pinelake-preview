Willie Park Jr. won the Open Championship twice before he ever drew a routing — and his design instincts are everywhere at Pine Lake: greens that reward thought over muscle, fairways that follow the land, and lake views that make you forget your scorecard.

The 2024 renovation — part of the club's $22 million investment — restored Park's character while modernizing conditions, tee to green.
