Thank you for the notes. Everything has been consolidated and the site is
updated — pinelake-preview.vercel.app. Here's where each item landed.

## Done

- **Anna Zappa replaces Aleksa Bissonette** throughout. Event and wedding
  inquiries now route to Anna, and the Events page carries her name, email
  and extension directly, so a prospect reaches a person rather than a form.
- **New junior golf photo** on the homepage and the Golf page. Worth noting:
  the old shot was in five other places we hadn't flagged — About, Contact,
  Events and two search pages. All seven are updated and the old file is gone.
- **Hero images lightened** site-wide. The photography reads through now
  instead of going dark behind the headlines.
- **Drummond testimonial** corrected to members since 1993.
- **Junior programming** now reads "Rookies (age 4–8) & Swim Team — all
  summer long."
- **Automotive heritage brought forward** on the About page. The club is
  described as what it was founded as: a weekend retreat for the men building
  the automobile.
- **Contact page** now lists three role-specific contacts — membership,
  catering and sales, and the GM. No full leadership listing, so it stays
  useful and doesn't need updating every time someone changes roles.
- **Lake living** is emphasized more strongly, on the Membership page in
  particular.

## Done differently than asked

- **The photo band on the Membership page** is gone, as requested. Rather
  than swap in another photograph, it's now a quiet typographic band about
  life on the water, with a line drawing of Pine Lake's actual shoreline and
  the club marked on the north shore. It does the job the photo was meant to
  do, and it's something no other club can reuse.
- **Lake-living language** deliberately avoids the "up north, close to home"
  framing. Another club nearby already owns that line, and echoing it would
  make us sound like the follower. The angle we're taking is that the lake
  isn't the view here — it's the address.

## Not changing

- **The Venues photograph on the Dining page.** We reviewed the alternative
  and are keeping what's there.
- **"The only automotive club" claim.** We can't substantiate it, so the
  heritage is stated plainly without the superlative. The founders' names do
  more work than the claim would.
