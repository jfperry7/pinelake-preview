Membership

Become part of something that has mattered for over 120 years.

Membership at Pine Lake isn't a transaction — it's a welcome. Here's what belonging makes possible, and how the conversation begins.

What Membership Makes Possible

Six things you'll find here — and nowhere else quite like this.

I.

Heritage & Legacy

A club founded in 1902 by Detroit's automotive pioneers — and a story your family becomes part of.

II.

Lakeside Living

395 acres of Pine Lake — private beach, marina, pool, swim platform, and sunset cruises.

III.

Championship Golf

Eighteen holes by Willie Park Jr., two-time British Open champion — restored and refined in our 2024 course renovation.

IV.

Tennis & Racquets

Nine courts, pickleball, and a renovated fitness center — the only full-service club in Southeast Michigan where the game never has to stop for winter.

V.

Table & Taste

Seven waterfront dining areas — from white-tablecloth evenings to burgers at the beach.

VI.

Community & Belonging

A club that celebrates families — where kids grow up, friendships deepen, and traditions take root.

How Joining Works

A personal process, from the very first hello.

There's no application portal and no fine print up front. Joining Pine Lake begins the way membership continues — personally. Our Membership Director walks with you every step of the way.

Step One

A conversation

Tell us about your family and what you're hoping to find. We'll answer every question — including the specifics, handled personally and directly.

Step Two

A private tour

Walk the course, see the lake, tour the renovated clubhouse — with your kids, at your pace, on a day that works for you.

Step Three

A warm welcome

When it feels right, we make it official — and your family's first season at the lake begins.
