About & Heritage

Discovered by pioneers. Built by families.

More than 120 years on the shores of Pine Lake — from the Automobile Club of Detroit to the premier family country club experience in Metro Detroit.

Our Story

A century and a quarter, in five chapters.

1902

Founded by Detroit's pioneers

Detroit's automotive pioneers — John Dodge, Ransom Olds, and Henry Ford among them — discover the shores of Pine Lake and found a club as a retreat from the city they were transforming.

1921

The club takes its name

The club takes the name of its lake — and its identity as a place for families, sport, and summers on the water.

The course

Willie Park Jr. lays out eighteen holes

Two-time British Open champion Willie Park Jr. lays out eighteen championship holes along the lake — a golden-age design the club still plays today. Laid out between 1917 and 1924.

2024

The largest investment in club history

The most significant investment in club history transforms the clubhouse and course — honoring 120 years of heritage while building for the next generation.

Today

The next chapter

The premier family country club experience in Metro Detroit — and a new generation of families writing the next chapter.

The Club Today

A history is only worth having if you are still adding to it.

The largest investment in club history rebuilt the clubhouse, the pool and the dining terrace, added new locker rooms and a youth room, and expanded the kitchen — then went out and renovated the golf course. What follows is not a plan. It is what is open now.

  • GolfEighteen Willie Park Jr. holes, two TrackMan simulators for the winter, and more than 120 events a year.
  • RacquetsNine courts — four of them heated and indoors, so the season never actually ends. Pickleball year-round.
  • AquaticsAn Olympic-sized pool with dedicated lap lanes, a private beach, a floating swim platform and a members-only marina.
  • FitnessA renovated fitness center: cardio, weights, TRX, a matrix multi-station, circuit machines and a yoga and stretching room.
  • The tableSeven waterfront settings, from white tablecloths to the Snack Shack, March through December.
What We Believe

Warm. Refined. Rooted. Active. Personal. That's how Pine Lake has always felt — and how it always will.
