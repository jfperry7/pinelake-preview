What You Get

Everything the city can't give you, 35 minutes away.

Championship Golf

18 holes by two-time British Open champion Willie Park Jr., renovated in 2024 — plus TrackMan simulators for the winter.

395 Acres of Lake

395-acre Pine Lake with a private beach, members-only marina, Olympic-sized pool, and sunset cruises.

Tennis All Winter

Nine tennis courts including four indoor Deco-Turf courts — the only full-service club in Southeast Michigan playing all winter.

Seven Waterfront Rooms

Seven dining areas with lake views from every one of them — client dinners, family Tuesdays, and beachside lunches.
