Dining & Social

Seven settings. One extraordinary view.

Every dining room at Pine Lake looks out on the water — four inside, three more out in the open air. The only question is what kind of evening you're in the mood for.

The Venues

From white tablecloths to bare feet in the sand.

The Occasion

Fine dining over the water, in the Formal Dining Room or the Lake Room.

The Everyday

The Patio is the retreat: open air, the full menu, and a straight view down Pine Lake.

The Summer

A drink on the Bluestone Patio, and the Snack Shack for feet that are still wet.

The same menu runs in every venue but the Snack Shack, which keeps its own short list. Expect member favorites alongside rotating specials from Chef Patrick and his crew — and dietary requests accommodated; just ask your server or the chef.

On a warm afternoon the Patio and the Bluestone Patio are the reason to come early: the same kitchen and the same menu, out in the open, with Pine Lake in front of you.

The Kitchen

Rebuilt for both kinds of night.

The renovation did not stop at the dining rooms. The kitchen behind them was overhauled into a state-of-the-art facility and expanded by 1,500 square feet, built to carry an a la carte dinner service and a large-scale event on the same evening — without either one giving ground to the other.

It is the reason a Tuesday dinner for two and a reception for two hundred come out of the same room, at the same standard.

Meet The Chef

Chef Patrick and his crew.

Executive Chef Patrick Srock leads the kitchen — the member favorites that never come off the menu, and a rotating slate of specials where the crew gets to stretch.

The menu leans on what is close by: Michigan beef, Great Lakes perch, local sweet corn in the weeks it is worth having.
