Why Pine Lake

A 120-year legacy, renewed for the next generation.

Founded in 1902 as the Automobile Club of Detroit — discovered by John Dodge, Ransom Olds, and Henry Ford — Pine Lake has spent more than a century perfecting one idea: a club where families come first. In 2024, we completed a $22 million clubhouse and course renovation, the most significant investment in our history. And when the season turns, indoor tennis and TrackMan golf keep the club going all winter.
